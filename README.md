## About

I build production AI products and the backend systems behind them. Over 4 years I have shipped 55+ projects across GenAI SaaS, agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, Web3 security tooling, and real-time platforms, and I care as much about architecture as I do about speed.

- Backend Engineer at **Diamond Bayou** (remote)
- Team Lead at **[CodeBuddy](https://codebuddy-e1xs.vercel.app/)**, an AI-powered studio that takes businesses from validated idea to launched, automated product
- Founder of **[VulnGuard AI](https://thevulnguardai.tech)**, an AI smart contract vulnerability scanner
- Computer Science student at **UET Lahore** (Batch 2023), based in Pakistan
- Working on multi-agent orchestration, LLM infrastructure, and low-latency real-time pipelines
